## Apple guidance applied

- System appearance remains the default, with explicit overrides available for people who need them.
- Liquid Glass is limited to window surfaces, cards, controls, and the Settings modal rather than applied indiscriminately.
- `prefers-reduced-transparency` disables blur and returns to solid surfaces.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables transitions and animations.
